    Scientific status

    The submission, validation and reporting workflow is implemented. Some challenge-specific metrics still need definitions.

    Implemented

    • DCE: Ktrans required; vp, ve and Kep optional; modelled signal-time required as 4-D. A methods document is asked about at upload and recorded, but not required — the challenge leads said it is not needed for the runs being done now.
    • Synthetic and clinical datasets remain one submission, with participant, repeat and site structure validated.
    • Within-scan ASL CBF/ATT and DCE Ktrans ROI statistics: mean, median, population SD, range, and coefficient of variation.
    • DCE measured-versus-modelled 4-D signal analysis: raw voxelwise Residual Sum of Squares (RSS), with median, mean, population SD and voxel count summaries when a measured signal is available.
    • Provisional descriptive grouping of scan-level ROI medians by participant, repeat and site, including SD, CoV and a signed difference for two clearly matched repeats or sites.
    • ASL: CBF and ATT maps required, with generic reference comparison where compatible reference data exists.
    • DSC: CBV, CBF and MTT maps required.
    • User-facing exports: blinded PDF, blinded HTML, blinded CSV Results, blinded JSON Results, ROI Parameter-map Statistics CSV, and an Unblinded CSV for organisers.

    Awaiting definition

    • The exact formula for a single official accuracy metric and for deviance.
    • Whether RSS should remain raw or be normalized.
    • Whether final repeat/site variability should be ROI-median based, voxelwise, or both, and which ICC model to use if ICC is required.
    • Formal thresholds, pass/fail rules, participant ranking, final ASL 4-D comparison requirements, and the final ROI/mask set.

    The prototype implements independently definable metrics now. Final challenge-specific formulas, thresholds and ranking rules will be added only after confirmation by the challenge leads. See What is left to do.

    QC and previews are available for readable maps. DCE Ktrans ROI descriptive statistics can appear when compatible ROI masks are available, generic reference comparisons when compatible reference maps are available, and provider-specific analysis or scoring when configured. Official OSIPI challenge ranking is not currently configured.
