    Scientific status

    The submission, validation and reporting workflow is implemented. Some challenge-specific metrics still need definitions.

    Implemented

    • DCE: Ktrans required; vp, ve and Kep optional; modelled signal-time required as 4-D; methods document required.
    • Synthetic and clinical datasets remain one submission, with participant, repeat and site structure validated.
    • Within-scan ROI Ktrans statistics: median, population SD, coefficient of variation.
    • ASL: CBF and ATT maps required, with generic reference comparison where compatible reference data exists.
    • DSC: CBV, CBF and MTT maps required.
    • User-facing exports: blinded PDF, blinded HTML, blinded CSV Results, blinded JSON Results, ROI Ktrans Statistics CSV, and an Unblinded CSV for organisers.

    Awaiting definition

    • The exact formula for accuracy and deviance.
    • Inter-participant, inter-repeat and inter-site SD and CoV, and whether they aggregate scan-level ROI medians or voxel values.
    • Residual sum of squares, which needs the measured dynamic signal in addition to the modelled one.
    • ASL 4-D fitted-model comparison, and the ICC model if ICC is required.

    Final challenge metrics are enabled after their formulas, inputs, masks and aggregation rules are confirmed by the challenge leads.

    QC and previews are available for readable maps. DCE Ktrans ROI descriptive statistics can appear when compatible ROI masks are available, generic reference comparisons when compatible reference maps are available, and provider-specific analysis or scoring when configured. Official OSIPI challenge ranking is not currently configured.
